Why the studio exists

Art Studio 184 was founded from a genuine passion for making. We are interested not only in producing an object, but in understanding what it should make people feel, how it will live in its space and what needs to be true for the result to work.

Every project is its own system

A sculpture for an exterior, a branded display for a launch and a one-night event installation cannot be treated the same way. Scale, transport, load, weather, finish, interaction and assembly all shape the solution. We choose the production route around those realities.

One team follows the work through

The project moves from discussion and visualization to modeling, structure, fabrication, finishing and installation. Keeping these stages connected helps protect the original idea and makes practical decisions faster and clearer.

What guides us

Four working principles

Not slogans — decisions we use when a project becomes complicated.

01

Attention

Small details affect whether an object feels convincing, finished and intentional.

02

Clarity

We define the task, approvals, production stages and responsibilities before they become problems.

03

Material logic

We use the material and technology that fit the environment, scale, budget and expected lifespan.

04

Responsibility

The final object must not only look right. It must be transportable, installable and suitable for real use.
