How it works

A clear path from idea to object.

Enough structure to control the result, with room for the idea to evolve.

Brief & reference

You send a request, sketch, reference or raw idea. We clarify the purpose, dimensions, environment and expected result.

Concept & estimate

We discuss the solution, materials, production logic, timing and provide a project estimate.

Final approval

After the details are approved, production starts with a 50% deposit.

Production

The work moves through modeling, engineering, fabrication and finishing inside the studio.

Delivery & installation

We hand over or install the finished object. The remaining 50% is paid at completion.
